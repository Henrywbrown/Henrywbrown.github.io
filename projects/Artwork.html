Artwork

Below are just a few of my favorite art pieces. These pieces reflect my affinity for design and creativity. Understanding the principles of design is essential for any maker. A balanced design is both harmonious and effective.

Phoenix Cross

In the spring of 2024 I was asked by my High school to construct and art piece of my choosing to be sold in live auction at an annual fundraising Gala. I chose to create a physical sculpture as apposed to a flat piece. The phoenix was our high school mascot, and so I decided to create a piece including one. I used scrap iron and wood that I salvaged from a decommissioned piano to create it. It sold for $2500 at auction.

Luther Seal

In December 2025 I was asked by a member of my church to create an art piece to be sold in a silent auction to raise money for the school children. I chose to create a Luther Seal, an important symbol in the Lutheran Church. The metal was from a old filing cabinet that the school had and the wood was from a decommissioned piano from the school. The piece sold for $400.

Charon

Completed as a part of a high school art course, this piece is charcoal on paper. It depicts a classmate posing as the mythological ferryman of the river Styx, Charon. The piece earned me a $100 award and is framed in the school hallways along with other award-winning student art.

Metal Rose

A metal rose I made to impress my girlfriend (it worked). It is made from layers of metal welded to 1/4" round stock. The petals were bent and beat into shape and the roundstock was cold forged to imitate natural materials. Inscribed on the leaf is: "Your mind is a garden" something she had previously claimed about me.

2D Design Coursework

This piece was completed as a part of a college art course. It is permanent marker on paper. The full design was created by taking one design, and inverting, mirroring, or inverting and mirroring accordingly to create a full piece.
